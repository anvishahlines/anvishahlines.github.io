About

Anvi Stevens is a practicing multimedia artist and painter based in Wakefield, Massachusetts.

Her studio practice explores the intersection of traditional painting techniques and modern textural mediums. Alongside her studio work, she serves as a High School Art Teacher at Mystic Valley Regional Charter School in Malden, MA, where she emphasizes hands-on visual literacy and material exploration for the next generation of creators.
